# Historical research

These materials are preserved for reference. They are not the evidence packages for the current published articles; use the [research index](../README.md) to find those.

| Package | Status | Public evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [agent-verification](agent-verification/README.md) | Earlier exploratory study; no current published article uses it | Study rules, methodology, aggregate results, a historical figure and an offline aggregate check |

The archived agent-verification study asked whether a separate coding agent could safely approve another agent's patch. It is different from the published [test-evidence study](../ai-reviewers-test-evidence/README.md), which compared reviewers' reactions to supplied reports. Do not pool their tasks or interpret their findings as one experiment.

Archiving preserves the evidence and its limits; it does not promote the study to a current result or enable an end-to-end rerun. No credentials, original execution environment or raw provider traces are included.
