# Historical scope note

The unchanged September 27, 2026 protocol links here for its original scope boundary: a local, zero-external-spend simulation, with publication and additional studies excluded from that initial phase.

The author subsequently approved publication of this completed study and its reproducibility package on October 13, 2026. The protocol remains unchanged so its original pre-run hash can be verified. Internal coordination records are not included in this public package.
