How sensitive are our evaluation findings?

We varied missing grades, the improvement required to switch, and how strongly questions are related. Each setting contains 2,000 evaluations. This review shows three representative approaches; the full tables below retain all six.

These settings reuse outcomes or random draws. They are paired comparisons, not 46,000 independent new experiments. Short vertical lines show pointwise 95% Monte Carlo intervals, not production-risk guarantees. Line segments connect tested settings; intermediate values were not tested.

A different switching requirement changes the decision

At a true +2-point gain, a switch is warranted under a one-point requirement but not under the two- or five-point requirements. Switches in the −4-point panel are always incorrect; those in the +10-point panel are correct. Do not read every downward line as a worse method.

Relatedness changes how much independent evidence we have

The true gain equals the two-point requirement, so switches do not clear the strict hurdle. The three dependence settings correspond to approximately 4.5%, 18.0% and 40.5% within-group correlation of paired differences. This panel uses a smaller vertical range to make the differences legible.
